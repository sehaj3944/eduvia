Privacy Policy & Student Data Charter

Last Updated: October 2026 • Valid for all Eduvia Clearinghouse interfaces under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

shield The Eduvia Anti-Spam & Zero-Broker Guarantee

Eduvia is built on a strict student-first charter. We never sell, lease, or distribute your contact details to third-party telemarketers, lead aggregators, or unauthorized commission brokers. All interactions remain strictly between you and verified university admissions offices.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate degree recommendations, tuition EMI calculations, and counseling appointments, Eduvia collects:

  • Voluntary Identity Information: Name, phone number, email address, and city submitted when requesting academic counseling or prospectus downloads.
  • Academic Background: Highest completed qualification, stream of interest, preferred study mode (100% online / hybrid), and career goals inputted in the pathway finder.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP address, device type, browser fingerprint, and anonymized user journeys to optimize comparison tools and page performance.

2. How Your Data is Used

Your data is utilized exclusively for:

  • Generating personalized university eligibility reports and fee comparisons.
  • Scheduling 1-on-1 counseling slots with UGC-certified education advisors.
  • Providing 0% EMI financing estimates through partnered NBFCs upon your explicit opt-in.
  • Sending statutory regulatory notices regarding UGC-DEB gazette updates.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Eduvia uses minimal, strictly essential cookies and browser LocalStorage to save your active degree shortlist, comparison matrix items, and UI theme preferences. We do not use third-party cross-site surveillance cookies.

4. Data Retention & Erasure (Your Rights)

Under Section 12 of the DPDP Act, you retain full rights over your data:

Right to Access & Correct

Request a copy of all information associated with your profile at any time.

Right to Erasure (Forget Me)

Email privacy@eduvia.in to permanently delete all counseling records within 48 business hours.
