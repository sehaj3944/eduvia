Admissions & Eligibility

WHAT TO CHECK BEFORE YOU APPLY

Understanding admission procedures, digital document verification, Academic Bank of Credits (ABC ID / APAAR), and stream prerequisites ensures a seamless enrollment process.

1. Academic Bank of Credits (ABC ID)

Mandatory APAAR/ABC ID linked to DigiLocker for tracking academic course credits across UGC-recognized Indian institutions.

2. Minimum Percentage Benchmarks

Undergraduate programmes typically require 45%–50% aggregate in 10+2. Postgraduate programmes require 50% in bachelor's degree (45% for reserved categories).

3. Specific Mathematics / Stream Criteria

Technical MCA and Data Science degrees require Mathematics/Statistics at graduation or 10+2, or bridging coursework.

Delivery Formats

UNDERSTANDING STUDY MODES

A factual comparative analysis of Online, On-Campus, and Hybrid learning models to help you select the structure that matches your career schedule.

Comparison Factor ONLINE ON-CAMPUS HYBRID / COHORT
Learning Environment 100% digital Learning Management System (LMS) with recorded videos and digital sandbox labs. Physical lecture halls, departmental libraries, and on-campus seminar rooms. Digital lectures combined with periodic weekend campus immersion bootcamps.
Schedule Flexibility High flexibility; asynchronous 24/7 video access with live weekend faculty doubt clinics. Fixed daily timetable; mandatory 75% physical attendance requirements. Moderate flexibility; self-paced weekday study with scheduled weekend cohorts.
Campus Access Virtual campus portal, digital library, and online student discussion forums. Full physical campus amenities, hostels, labs, and student recreation centres. Hybrid access; access to campus facilities during designated workshop weekends.
Delivery Format Four-quadrant e-learning model (e-Tutorial, e-Content, Discussion Forum, Assessment). In-person chalk-and-talk, physical laboratory work, and classroom presentations. Blended modular format integrating online modules with executive masterclasses.
Student Interaction Pan-India digital cohorts, peer groups, discussion boards, and virtual study circles. Daily face-to-face peer collaboration and physical student club activities. Virtual networking supplemented with in-person executive networking weekends.
Examination Format Remote AI-monitored & human-proctored web-based examinations from home. Physical paper-pen or computer lab examinations at designated university halls. Online semester tests with select on-campus practical capstone assessments.

Statutory Standards

KNOW WHAT YOU'RE CHOOSING

Distinguishing university-level recognition from programme-specific approvals and delivery-mode entitlements.

Statutory Equivalence verified

UGC-DEB Entitlement

The Distance Education Bureau (DEB) of the University Grants Commission specifically approves universities to deliver degree programmes via online or ODL modes with full equivalence to regular degrees.

Mandatory for all Indian online degree programmes.
Institutional Quality grade

NAAC Accreditation (A++ / A+)

The National Assessment and Accreditation Council evaluates curriculum, teaching quality, and infrastructure. Institutions need a minimum NAAC score of 3.26+ for online entitlement.

Evaluates 7 comprehensive institutional quality metrics.
National Ranking bar_chart

NIRF Ranking Framework

The Ministry of Education ranks universities across Teaching, Learning & Resources (TLR), Research & Professional Practice (RP), Graduation Outcomes (GO), and Outreach.

Top 100 universities eligible for online degree delivery.
Technical Oversight school

AICTE Approvals

The All India Council for Technical Education regulates management (MBA/PGDM) and technical computing programmes (MCA) to ensure curriculum relevance.

Applies to technical & management degree tracks.

Quick Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Factual answers to key questions prospective students evaluate before choosing a degree programme.

You can evaluate universities using the Universities Directory and the Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix. Compare institutions across statutory accreditations (UGC-DEB, NAAC, NIRF), total tuition costs, semester installment options, faculty credentials, and examination models.
Visit the Programmes Directory to filter by degree level (UG/PG), academic stream, specialisation, and monthly EMI. You can add up to 4 programmes to your active comparison tray to inspect side-by-side curricular and financial parameters.
Always verify: 1) Valid UGC-DEB recognition for the specific university and academic session; 2) Minimum eligibility percentages and 10+2 / bachelor's stream requirements; 3) Total all-inclusive fees including semester exam charges; 4) AI-proctored examination hardware requirements (webcam and microphone).
Postgraduate management degrees (MBA) generally require a bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). Technical master's degrees (MCA, M.Sc Data Science) require BCA, B.Sc Computer Science, or graduation with Mathematics/Statistics at graduation or intermediate level.
UGC-DEB provides statutory recognition ensuring online degrees are equivalent to regular degrees for government jobs and higher studies. NAAC assesses institutional academic and infrastructural quality (A++ being highest). NIRF provides Ministry of Education performance rankings.
Online programmes provide 100% digital LMS delivery and schedule flexibility without daily campus attendance, ideal for working professionals. On-campus programmes require daily physical attendance and campus facilities. Both hold statutory degree equivalence under UGC regulations when earned from approved institutions.
